# OneConnect — National Citizen Benefit Navigator & Unified Welfare Portal

OneConnect is an AI-powered Citizen Benefit Navigator and Unified Welfare Portal built to bridge the awareness gap between Indian citizens and government welfare schemes, official civic documents, and government career opportunities.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Life Situation Navigator**: Describe life events in plain language (voice or text in 11 Indian languages) to discover matching welfare schemes.
- **250+ Verified Welfare Schemes**: Grounded in official `.gov.in` portals covering Agriculture, Healthcare (PM-JAY), Housing (PMAY, PM Surya Ghar), Education, Women & Child Development (PMMVY, Sukanya Samriddhi), MSME (Udyam, Mudra), and Social Security.
- **Document Readiness & Application Guides**: Comprehensive checklist and step-by-step guides for Aadhaar, PAN, e-Shram, ABHA, DigiLocker, Caste/Income/EWS Certificates, and Passports.
- **Government Jobs & Recruitment**: Real-time notifications and eligibility matching for SSC, UPSC, Railway, Banking, and Defense examinations.
- **Household Benefit Roadmap**: Family profile manager that computes cumulative household entitlements and optimized application sequences.
- **Application Tracker**: Track submitted welfare applications across their approval lifecycles.
- **AI Citizen Assistant**: 24/7 interactive conversational assistant with domain grounding.
